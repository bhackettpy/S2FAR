Aero-Propulsive Model Validation for the Dufour AM10 Mini Tiltwing

Dufour Aerospace and Oklahoma State University are collaborating under Dufour internal R&D funding to gather experimental data for testing aero-propulsive models in the OSU-Tulsa WindShaper. Led by Dr. Anthony Comer, the project uses the S2F-AM193, a direct 2/3-scale model of the Dufour AM10 Mini tiltwing aircraft.

The effort begins with isolated-propeller tests that characterize propeller forces and moments as functions of advance ratio and incidence angle. Wing-mounted-propeller tests then quantify how a downstream wing changes propeller loading. Flow visualization is used to study slipstream-wing interactions, including wake contraction, skew, and curvature along the blown portions of the wing.

The final phase generates longitudinal aero-propulsive coefficient maps for the wing and full aircraft as functions of slipstream thrust coefficient and wing tilt angle. Testing also compares co-rotating propellers with configurations using reversed inner-propeller rotation. A VISTA hardware-ready model supports the experimental campaign and connects the S2FAR Lab's simulation and flight-test capabilities with the WindShaper test environment.

Expected outcomes include technical data and reports documenting the S2F-AM193 test article, load cells and experimental setups, test methods, and results from the isolated-propeller, wing-mounted-propeller, flow-visualization, and longitudinal coefficient-map experiments.

Sponsor/Partner Dufour Aerospace
Project Period June 1-November 30, 2026
OSU Leadership Dr. Anthony Comer (PI)
Research Platform S2F-AM193, a direct 2/3-scale model of the Dufour AM10 Mini tiltwing
Facility 10×8 OSU-Tulsa WindShaper
Total Project Budget $15,065
  • Deploy a VISTA hardware-ready model for WindShaper experiments
  • Conduct isolated-propeller force and moment testing
  • Conduct wing-mounted-propeller testing with a downstream wing
  • Characterize performance versus advance ratio and incidence angle
  • Visualize slipstream contraction, skew, and curvature
  • Generate longitudinal aero-propulsive coefficient maps versus slipstream thrust coefficient and wing tilt angle
  • Compare co-rotating and reversed inner-propeller configurations
  • Document the test article, instrumentation, methods, data, and results
