Simulation-to-Flight Development and Testing for Advanced Multirotor Aircraft

The S2FAR Lab is collaborating with Surgeon Drone to advance the simulation, integration, and experimental evaluation of an advanced multirotor aircraft. The project combines isolated component testing with VISTA digital-twin development and hardware-ready flight-control implementation. Motors, propellers, electronic speed controllers, batteries, and supporting electronics are evaluated through bench and load-cell testing to characterize propulsion-system behavior and provide data for model development.

The team is also developing Pixhawk-based control and avionics implementations with behavior traceable to ArduPilot, including the sensor, peripheral, power-distribution, and communication interfaces needed for simulation-to-hardware operation. Candidate quadrotor, hexacopter, octocopter, and coaxial X8 architectures are being evaluated through performance simulation and subscale technical demonstrators.

Guidance and flight testing of later-stage demonstrators will proceed as hardware readiness, test-site access, weather, and safety reviews allow. The effort supports a rigorous simulation-to-flight workflow that connects propulsion characterization, digital-twin development, controls integration, and incremental flight-test validation to accelerate the development of advanced multirotor systems.
