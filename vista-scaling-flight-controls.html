VISTA: Scaling Flight Controls and Handling Qualities for eVTOL Aircraft

Subscale aircraft offer a practical way to investigate advanced aircraft configurations, but interpreting their results for larger vehicles requires suitable scaling methods. The proposed VISTA project—VTOL Integrated Scaling, Testing, and Handling Qualities Analysis—brings together Systems Technology, Inc. (STI), Oklahoma State University, the University of Portland, and Dufour Aerospace to develop and evaluate a unified approach to scaling flight control behavior and handling qualities.

S2FAR's contribution includes selecting a representative subscale aircraft, developing a MATLAB/Simulink digital twin, supporting flight control design, and conducting flight evaluations. The team will study how aircraft dimensions, mass properties, response characteristics, maneuver timing, and pilot cueing affect meaningful comparisons across scales. Candidate controllers will be evaluated in simulation and deployed on both a Pixhawk Cube Orange+ and STI's research Flight Control Unit. Scaled mission task element courses will support repeatable assessments of vehicle performance and pilot ratings.

Phase I is intended to produce initial scaling methods, subscale flight-test evidence, comparisons with available industry data, and a preliminary concept and fabrication plan for a future full-scale demonstrator. Construction and full-scale validation belong to a potential Phase II effort. The long-term aim is to make subscale testing more useful for evaluating flight controls and handling qualities during eVTOL development.
